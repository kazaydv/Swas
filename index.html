Why your mouth is the problem

Open-mouth sleep is a quiet tax on your whole day

  • Dry mouth & morning breath

    Air bypasses your nose's natural filtering and humidifying, drying out your mouth by sunrise.

  • Snoring, most nights

    A slack, open jaw is one of the most common mechanical causes of snoring — for you, or whoever's next to you.

  • Shallower sleep cycles

    Mouth breathing is linked to more restless, fragmented sleep — even when you don't remember waking up.

  • A foggy next day

    Lower oxygen exchange overnight tends to show up the next afternoon, as low energy and thin focus.

The oval isn't decoration — it's the safety design

Full mouth tape can feel restrictive for a lot of first-time users. SWAS is cut differently on purpose: the open oval centre guides your mouth closed, rather than sealing it shut.

You can still part your lips to speak, cough, or breathe through the gap if you need to — the patch simply removes the default, so your nose does the work instead.

What's actually happening

Your nose is the better airway — it's just easy to skip

  • Filters & warms the air

    Nasal passages trap dust and allergens and humidify air before it reaches your lungs.

  • Supports oxygen uptake

    Nasal breathing is associated with nitric oxide release, which can aid how efficiently oxygen is absorbed.

  • Encourages deeper breaths

    It naturally shifts you toward slower, diaphragmatic breathing instead of shallow chest breathing.

  • Hypoallergenic, fragrance-free

    Dermatologically tested adhesive, latex-free, with no harsh residue on removal.
